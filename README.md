# ai-classroom

Educational material that explains AI: its concepts, its history and the terms that come with it. Each folder
is one self-contained project for a specific audience.

| Project | What it teaches |
|---|---|
| [`ai-terminology-hell/`](ai-terminology-hell/) | "A Short History of AI: From Generative AI to Agentic AI", a slide deck that explains the most important AI terms (ML, LLM, RAG, MCP, agents, …) to a non-technical audience by telling the story of how AI evolved. |
